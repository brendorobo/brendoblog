I've gone down a rabbit hole I don't want to climb out of: running large language models locally, on my own
hardware, fully under my control.

## Where it started

It started with LM Studio and Llama. Pulling down a model and running it on my home PC, no cloud, no account,
no data leaving the house, hooked me immediately. Then I got into agents, including the ones PewDiePie showed
off in his local-AI setup, Hermes and Odysseus, which pushed me past just chatting with a model.

## My own private AI

Now I host an open-source Gemma model on my home PC as my own private AI, and I'm building out an agent
workflow aimed at cybersecurity, something to help me practice running detections and work through
investigations. That project is where I learned what an MCP server actually is and how agents talk to tools
and data behind the scenes.

<Callout type="note" title="the stack so far">
LM Studio · Llama · Gemma (self-hosted) · agents · MCP servers
</Callout>

## Why local?

The real reason I keep at it is privacy. I care about where my data goes, and running everything locally means
I stay in control of it instead of handing it to someone else. This space moves fast, and I'm always chasing
the next bit of AI news to keep up.
